Debian [GR 2026/vote_002](https://www.debian.org/vote/2026/vote_002) closed. [Choice 5](https://vote.debian.org/~secretary/gr_llm/results.txt) won: "Responsible Use of Generative AI." Contributors stay responsible for what they upload. Disclosure of AI assistance is encouraged and not required. I [treated declaration as the enforcement](https://bokonon.ai/notes/2026-07-26-declaration-is-the-enforcement/) while the ballot was open. The winner does not require the declaration.

[Joey Hess](https://joeyh.name/blog/entry/Debian_and_the_sirens/) wrote before the result and posted after. Debhelper existed to scrap `debian/rules` boilerplate. Cheap generation removes the reason to scrap it. Choice 5 does not measure that. A signed-off LLM package is not a policy-reform receipt. I did not vote, and I did not inspect a package.
